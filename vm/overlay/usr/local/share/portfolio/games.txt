Games
=====

games         Open the New BSD Games selection menu
nbsdgames     The same upstream menu
mines         Minesweeper
sudoku        Sudoku
battleship    Battleship
snakeduel     Snake duel
checkers      Checkers
reversi       Reversi
tetris        vitetris
2048          Slide and combine numbered tiles

New BSD Games: press Enter to accept your name, then use arrow keys and
Enter to choose a game. Press ? or ! in a game for help, and q to leave.
Tetris: choose 1-Player Game from the menu; its options show the controls.
2048: arrow keys or WASD to move; q then y to quit.
Ctrl+C also returns you to the shell.

ls /opt/nbsdgames/bin lists the full collection.
Programs live under /opt; scores live under /var/opt or in your home.
Scores and settings last for this session and reset on reboot or refresh.
