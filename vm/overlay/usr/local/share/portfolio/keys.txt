Keyboard shortcuts
==================

Tab                      Complete a command or path
Up / Down                Recall previous commands
Ctrl+L                   Clear the display
Ctrl+C                   Stop the current program (no text selected)
Shift+Tab                Move focus out of the terminal

Copy and paste
--------------

Copy selected text with Ctrl+C or Ctrl+Shift+C.
Paste with Ctrl+V or Ctrl+Shift+V. On Mac, use Cmd+C and Cmd+V.
The browser context menu also supports copy and paste.

Reading and editing
-------------------

In less: Space advances a page, / searches, and q exits.
In nano: Ctrl+O saves, Enter confirms, and Ctrl+X exits.
